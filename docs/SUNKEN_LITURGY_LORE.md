# The Sunken Liturgy
### *The Ledger of Oaths & The Cosmic Debt*

> *"In the first silence, we took our first breath on credit. And now, the Auditors have come to collect."*  
> — Litany of the Blank Page, Verse I

---

## I. The Genesis: The Ledger of Oaths

Before the stars were named and before flesh learned to bleed, reality was an insolvent void. The **First Arbiters** did not speak creation into being through divine love or elemental force; they drafted it as a **Cosmic Loan**. 

Every breath of warm air, every spark of thought, every cradle of memory, and every drop of sunlight was borrowed capital—drawn from an incomprehensible reservoir of primeval potential. In exchange, humanity signed an unwritten, binding covenant: at the end of time, all borrowed vitality was to be repaid in full, with interest, back into the **Grand Ledger**.

To administer and enforce this cosmic contract, seven monumental debt-cities were erected across the subterranean abysses of the world, overseen by the **Arch-Creditors**—titanic, faceless entities wielding colossal balance scales and iron quills. For countless millennia, the balance held: life was lived under the quiet weight of an inescapable mortal invoice.

---

## II. The Collapse: When the Ink Ran Dry

The calamity began when the Grand Ledger ran out of ink.

Generations multiplied, oaths went unpaid, promises dissolved in wars and plagues, and the ledger balances drifted into insurmountable arrears. When the celestial accounting could no longer be balanced, natural death ceased to function. 

People who died with unpaid debts could no longer cross into the peaceful oblivion of the grave. Instead, their corpses were impounded by cosmic law, transforming them into **The Indebted**—hollow, tallow-skinned husks tethered to unfinished contracts. Driven by the cold imperative of their debts, they wander the flooded archives and crumbling counting houses, endlessly performing routine labor to pay off obligations their living descendants no longer even remember.

Worse still, the Arch-Creditors themselves grew desperate. To balance their cosmic ledgers, they began foreclosing on human souls before their time—harvesting living memories, first loves, and ancestral names as collateral. This cannibalistic consumption of human essence mutated the Arch-Creditors into grotesque, bloated, iron-clad monstrosities known as **The Auditors**. Armed with the power to rewrite the parchment of reality, the Auditors strike entire lineages and cities from history simply to liquidate their accounts.

---

## III. The Awaited One: The Forgotten Witness

Ancient apocrypha preserved in the lowest submerged catacombs speaks of a singularity in the contract: **The Forgotten Witness**.

The Witness was born outside the margins of the Grand Ledger—a child of an unrecorded birth whose name was never entered into the counting books. Having inherited no ancestral debt, the Forgotten Witness is entirely immune to the default-mutating curses that twist the minds of ordinary mortals.

Dispatched from the shadows by the **Order of the Blank Page**—a clandestine monastic order of heretical scholars who seek the total annihilation of the Ledger—the Forgotten Witness must delve into the sunken counting houses, slay the four mad Auditors, claim their **Four Sealed Ledgers**, and breach the **Vault of Settlement** to confront the final balance sheet of reality.

### The Four Sealed Ledgers:
1. **The Ledger of Usury** — Kept by the *High Bailiff of Arrears*, recording compound suffering and interest on stolen life.
2. **The Ledger of Mortmain** — Guarded by *The Twins of Surety*, enshrining eternal possession and dead-hand control over the drowned metropolis.
3. **The Ledger of Collateral** — Held by *Chief Auditor Malakor*, containing millions of harvested human memories pressed into brass plates.
4. **The Ledger of Foreclosure** — Wielded by *The Iron Forecloser*, granting the terrifying power to erase physical matter that fails inspection.

---

## IV. The Three Endings

When the Forgotten Witness breaches the inner sanctuary of the Vault of Settlement, where the bleeding core of the Grand Ledger pulses upon suspended brass scales, three choices determine the fate of existence:

### 1. The Default Cycle (The Re-Collateralization)
The Witness bows to the inevitability of the cosmic system. Recognizing that reality cannot hold together without its defining contract, the Witness offers their own immortal soul as the ultimate collateral, dipping their finger into their own blood to sign an extension for another era.  
*Outcome:* The world is saved from immediate collapse, but humanity is bound into an even more predatory, authoritarian age. Every child is born in heavy iron bonds, and the Auditors patrol every cradle with stricter quotas.

### 2. The Write-Off (The Great Bankruptcy)
The Witness draws a fiery brand and touches it to the parchment of the Grand Ledger. Millennia of ink, oaths, debts, and ledgers turn to cinders in seconds. Every contract is instantly liquidated, and every soul is freed from obligation.  
*Outcome:* With the contractual framework that held space, time, and matter together unraveled, reality gently and quietly dissolves. Mountains drift into weightless dust, oceans evaporate without sound, and humanity slips into a peaceful, obligation-free silence.

### 3. The Unbound Covenant (The Secret Truth)
By discovering all seven **Apocryphal Receipts** hidden across the world's most wretched outcasts and forgotten pariahs, the Witness does not burn or sign the ledger. Instead, using the *Quill of Default*, the Witness inscribes a revolutionary new clause across the front page: *Existence is an inherent right, not borrowed capital.*  
*Outcome:* The Ledger is purged of interest, debts, and creditors. The Auditors collapse as their brass armor shatters, the Indebted are released into true rest, and the living awaken into a world where life belongs solely to the one who breathes it.

## V. Key Figures & Bosses

### The High Bailiff of Arrears
* **Domain:** The Flooded Counting House of the Lower Reach
* **Visual:** A colossal iron-masked adjudicator draped in moldering crimson silk, carrying brass judgment scales in one gauntlet and a heavy, stone-weighted Gavel of Foreclosure in the other.
* **Special Mechanic — Interest-Burn:**  
  The Bailiff punishes players who attempt to heal recklessly. Whenever the player drinks from their flask while within the Bailiff's aura, the Bailiff bellows *"DEFAULT PENALTY IMPOSED!"*, inflicting searing **Interest-Burn** that drains health over time for every drop of vitality consumed.

### The Twins of Surety (Caelum & Vespera)
* **Domain:** The Suspended Scales of the Sunken Scriptorium
* **Visual:** Two ethereal, blindfolded twins connected by glowing brass chains that loop directly through their sternums, hovering above the watery floor.
* **Special Mechanic — Shared Equilibrium:**  
  The twins share a single connected ledger of poise and life. Striking one causes the other's balance scale to tilt; if their damage difference exceeds a set threshold, they unleash a room-wide shockwave of catastrophic reconciliation. Victory requires measured, balanced combat against both simultaneously.

### The Indebted Husks
* **Visual:** Shriveled corpses bound in parchment scrolls and sealing wax, dragging massive iron counting weights or wielding broken quill-blades. They murmur unfinished financial calculations and plead for forgiveness of debts long forgotten.

## VII. The Corpse Mechanic: The Receipt of Settlement

In the world of The Sunken Liturgy, death is treated as an acute default on one's vital balance:
1. Upon death, your body leaves behind not mere souls, but a glowing **Receipt of Settlement**.
2. If you perish before reclaiming this Receipt, **Compound Interest** begins to accrue against your soul, placing a decaying lien on your maximum vigor until you successfully redeem the contract.
3. Successfully touching your Receipt restores your full balance with a surge of renewed solvency: *"SOLVENCY RESTORED • OATH SETTLED"*.
