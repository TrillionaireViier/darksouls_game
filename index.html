I. THE GENESIS: THE LEDGER OF OATHS

Before the stars were named, reality was forged as a Ledger of Oaths by the First Arbiters. Every breath of warm air, every spark of memory, and every living name was borrowed capital—drawn from an incomprehensible reservoir of primeval cosmic potential.

In exchange, humanity signed an unwritten covenant: at the end of time, all vitality was to be repaid in full, with interest, back into the Grand Ledger. The seven sunken debt-cities were erected across subterranean abysses, watched over by the faceless Arch-Creditors.

II. THE COLLAPSE: THE DEFAULT CYCLE

The calamity began when the Grand Ledger ran out of ink. Overdue oaths accumulated across millenia, and when debts went unpaid, people stopped dying naturally.

They became The Indebted—hollow husks bound to unfinished contracts, scrubbing counting house flagstones to discharge debts their descendants no longer remember. In panic, the Arch-Creditors harvested living memories as collateral, mutating into bloated, iron-clad Auditors who rewrite reality to liquidate accounts.

III. THE AWAITED ONE: THE FORGOTTEN WITNESS

Prophecies foretold the arrival of the Forgotten Witness—an entity born outside the margins of the Ledger with zero inherited debt, completely immune to default curses.

Dispatched by the Order of the Blank Page, the Witness descends to hunt down the Four Sealed Ledgers (*Usury*, *Mortmain*, *Collateral*, *Foreclosure*) held by mad Auditors to reach the Vault of Final Settlement.

Sign your own soul as eternal collateral to extend the ledger for another era, binding mankind under harsher, more predatory contracts.

Burn the Grand Ledger to cancel all debt at once—unraveling the cosmic structure so reality quietly dissolves without obligation.

Collect the Apocryphal Receipts of Settlement to rewrite the terms of existence: reality is an inherent right, not borrowed debt.
